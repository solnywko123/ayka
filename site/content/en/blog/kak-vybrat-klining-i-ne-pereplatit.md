The cleaning services market in Bishkek is growing, and with it, the number of options — from independent cleaners found through classifieds to full-fledged companies with a website and an online calculator. The spread in price and quality is enormous. In this article — a practical checklist for what to look for when choosing a cleaning company, so you don't overpay or end up disappointed with the result.

## 1. Price transparency before work begins

The first and most important sign of a reliable company is the ability to find out an approximate cost before you even call, and an exact price before work starts — not "based on the actual outcome." If a company refuses to give even a rough price range without an in-person visit, that's a red flag: it often masks a desire to quote a low price verbally and then find a reason to raise it on site.

A good sign is a public price list on the website and a calculator where you can enter your apartment's details yourself and get an estimate. That doesn't mean the final price can never change at all — sometimes an apartment's condition differs from what the client described — but the range should be known in advance, not sprung on you as a surprise.

## 2. What exactly is included in the cleaning

Phrases like "we'll clean everything" say nothing about the actual scope of work. A reliable company provides a concrete checklist: exactly what's included in general or maintenance cleaning, and what's not included and has to be booked separately. This protects both sides from misunderstandings: you know exactly what to expect, and the cleaners know exactly what scope of work they need to complete in the agreed time.

Pay attention to whether the company is upfront about what the service doesn't cover. For example, a standard general cleaning usually doesn't include window washing or furniture dry cleaning — those are separate services. If a company hides such limitations and then demands extra payment for "additional" work that should have been obvious from the start, that's a warning sign.

## 3. Payment terms

Notice when the company asks for payment — before work starts, during the process, or after you've personally checked the result. Payment on acceptance of the work is a sign the company is confident in the quality of its service and isn't afraid the client will refuse to pay.

If a company insists on full prepayment with no quality guarantees whatsoever, weigh the risk: if you're unhappy with the result, getting a refund or a re-clean will be much harder.

## 4. Vetting and preparing cleaners

Cleaning means giving someone access to your home, often while you're not there. Ask the company how they vet cleaners before hiring: do they check documents, do they conduct interviews. A responsible company will answer this question without hesitation, down to specifics.

It's also worth asking whether the company works with a consistent staff or sends a different random contractor every time. A stable team of cleaners — especially if you're planning recurring cleaning — is an extra guarantee of predictable quality.

## 5. Willingness to redo the work

Even reliable companies have occasional slip-ups — human error is never fully eliminated. What matters is how the company responds to legitimate complaints. If the terms of service clearly state that, for objective shortcomings, the company will redo the problem areas free of charge within a reasonable timeframe (say, 24 hours), that's a sign of a responsible approach. The absence of such a policy, or vague promises to "figure it out individually" with no specifics, should be treated as a risk.

## 6. Reviews — with a critical eye

Reviews are useful, but it's important to view them critically. Pay attention not just to the overall rating, but to how specific the reviews actually are — do clients mention details (which service, which district, what exactly they liked or didn't). Overly generic, uniform reviews with no specifics can sometimes be fabricated.

Honest companies openly disclose when demo/placeholder reviews are temporarily shown on the site before a base of real reviews has built up, rather than passing off made-up stories as genuine.

## 7. Recurring vs. one-time bookings

If you need cleaning not just once but on a recurring basis, ask whether there's a discount for frequency and how it's calculated — weekly, biweekly and monthly visits usually get different discounts. It's also worth asking what happens if you need to reschedule or cancel a visit — whether there are any cancellation fees.

## Bottom line: a short checklist before you book

Before submitting a request to a cleaning company, check:

- Is there a transparent price list or calculator for estimating cost in advance;
- Is there a concrete checklist of what's included and what's not;
- When does payment happen — before, during, or after you check the result;
- Does the company vet cleaners before hiring them;
- Is there a policy of free re-cleaning for legitimate complaints;
- How specific and credible are the reviews on the site;
- Is there a discount for recurring bookings, if that's relevant to you.
