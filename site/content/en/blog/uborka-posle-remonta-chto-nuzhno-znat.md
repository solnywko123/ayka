The renovation is done, but the apartment looks worse than before you started: construction dust everywhere, paint drips on the window sills, glue streaks on the glass, cement dust in the tile grout. Anyone who's ever renovated in Bishkek knows this feeling. In this article we'll cover when it's best to book [post-renovation cleaning](/en/uslugi/uborka-posle-remonta/), what it costs, and how it fundamentally differs from a regular general cleaning.

## How post-renovation cleaning differs from general cleaning

At first glance it seems like the same thing — just a "thorough clean." In practice the difference is significant, and that's exactly why post-renovation cleaning costs more than general cleaning: the base rate per square meter here is noticeably higher.

Construction dust isn't ordinary household dust. It's finer, works its way deeper into surface pores, and requires several passes with professional equipment to actually be removed rather than just smeared around. On top of that, surfaces after a renovation often carry specific residue you won't see with regular cleaning: paint drips, spray-foam marks, cement splashes on tile, stickers and protective film on window glass, and streaks from painter's tape.

Post-renovation cleaning also simply takes more time: cleaners need to work on ventilation grilles, the tops of pipes and ducts, thoroughly wash out grout lines between floor tiles, and often haul out small construction debris — wallpaper scraps, material packaging, plastic film.

## When to book the cleaning

The optimal time to book post-renovation cleaning is 1–2 days after the dustiest stages — sanding, plastering and painting — are finished. Book too early, while dusty work is still ongoing, and the effect won't last — the dust will just settle again. Wait too long, and some residue, like paint drips, may harden further and take more effort to remove.

A good rule of thumb is to plan post-renovation cleaning for the stage when furniture has just been or is about to be delivered, but the apartment is still empty or nearly empty. This makes it much easier for cleaners to reach every surface, including entire floors, without having to work around furniture.

## What it costs and what it depends on

Post-renovation cleaning pricing follows the same principle as general cleaning: a base rate per square meter, plus a surcharge for extra bathrooms, plus the cost of add-ons like window washing (especially relevant after a renovation, since glass almost always has painter's tape residue and construction dust on it).

If the renovation was extensive — say, a full flooring replacement and wall plastering — plan for more time and a higher budget than for a cosmetic touch-up. ## What's not included in standard post-renovation cleaning

It's important to understand the boundaries of the service to avoid misunderstandings. Standard post-renovation cleaning doesn't include hauling away bulky construction waste — pallets, large drywall offcuts or pipe sections. That kind of waste is usually removed by the renovation crew or a separate hauling service before the cleaners arrive.

Complete removal of very old, deeply set-in stains also isn't guaranteed — if the renovation finished several months ago and surfaces have absorbed grime, the result should be assessed individually: sometimes complex cases need a different, stronger cleaning agent, and an honest company will warn you about this upfront rather than promise the impossible.

## Practical tips before the cleaners arrive

To make post-renovation cleaning as effective as possible, there are a few simple things worth doing beforehand:

- Remove any bulky construction waste and material packaging from the apartment, if that's not part of the booked service.
- Clear access to every room — put away tools and ladders if the renovation crew hasn't fully wrapped up yet.
- Ventilate the apartment before the visit — this helps fine dust settle faster and makes the cleaning more effective.
- Point out any areas with especially stubborn residue in the request comment — this lets the team bring the right products and tools in advance.
